Privacy policy

Karl, a private personal assistant. Last updated October 12, 2026.

Who uses Karl

Karl is used only by its owner's household. It is not offered to the public.

What Karl accesses

When the owner connects a Gmail account, Karl asks Google for two permissions:

  • Read email (gmail.readonly), to find and read messages the owner asks about and to notice new mail worth the owner's attention.
  • Compose email (gmail.compose), to save drafts and to send messages. Every message Karl sends is first shown to the owner in full, and is sent only after the owner approves it.

Karl does not delete, archive, or change existing messages.

How the information is used

Email is used only to help the owner with their own mail. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except as described below.

Karl's language understanding runs on an AI service. When Karl reads or drafts a message for the owner, the text needed for that task is sent to that service to produce the answer.

Karl's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Where it is stored

Sign-in tokens and the small records Karl keeps (such as which messages it has already checked) are stored on the owner's own computer.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect Karl at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Karl then loses access immediately.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email address shown on Google's sign-in screen for this app.
